    02 · Featured work

    Two systems I architected end to end

    2026 · SmartData Technologies Private codebase

    SmartSense: a multi-tenant IoT platform

    SmartData's own platform for every sensor and actuator product the company builds. An organisation signs in, registers sites, gateways and devices, gets MQTT credentials locked to its own topics, and sees live readings, charts, alerts and reports. Rules switch relays from live sensor data, safely.

    • Tenant isolation in four layers: per-organisation broker ACLs, server-verified sessions, Postgres row-level security and composite foreign keys, all covered by mutation-checked negative tests.
    • Automation engine: and/or condition trees across sensors, directional hysteresis, cooldowns, rule pairs, and "ask a person first" approvals.
    • Reconciliation: compares what automation intended with what the relay reported, and corrects drift after power cuts and reboots.
    • For operators: live push over Server-Sent Events, drag-and-drop dashboards, e-mail and Telegram alerts, immutable daily PDF reports, and a one-command installer for any Docker host.
    • 440automated tests
    • 108documented design decisions
    • 45schema migrations
