Cow Health Monitoring & Alert System

An IoT system that keeps watch over a cow's health using a heartbeat and oxygen-saturation sensor, an accelerometer, a waterproof temperature probe and a DHT11. It runs on the ESP32 Dev Kit v1, sends email alerts, and can be monitored and controlled remotely from a mobile app.

How it works

Several sensors work together to build a picture of the animal's condition. The ESP32 reads them, raises an email notification when something needs attention, and connects to a mobile app for remote monitoring and control of the system.

  • Heartbeat & oxygen saturation: a combined sensor tracks the cow's heart rate and SpO₂.
  • Movement: an accelerometer captures the animal's motion.
  • Body temperature: a waterproof temperature sensor.
  • Surroundings: a DHT11 measures ambient temperature and humidity.
  • Alerts & remote access: email notifications plus a mobile app for monitoring and control.
  • OTA updates: firmware can be updated wirelessly, without retrieving the device.
