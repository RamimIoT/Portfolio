Health & Safety

Patient Health Monitoring & Alert System

An IoT health monitoring system that tracks a patient's condition with several sensors and sends alerts through email notifications and a mobile app. It is built on the ESP32 Dev Kit v1, with an OLED display for local readings.

Type
Patient health monitoring
Platform
ESP32 Dev Kit v1
Alerts
Email notifications & mobile app
Firmware
Over-the-air (OTA) updates
  • ESP32 Dev Kit v1
  • Health sensors
  • OLED display
  • Wi-Fi
  • Email alerts
  • Mobile app
  • OTA

Key features

  • Continuous monitoring: multiple sensors track the patient's health.
  • Alerts: email notifications and a mobile app keep carers informed remotely.
  • Local display: an OLED screen shows the relevant readings at the bedside.
  • OTA updates: firmware can be updated wirelessly.
