Heavy Rain & Landslide Warning Device

An IoT device that detects heavy rainfall in landslide-prone areas of the Ukhiya Rohingya Camp and gives timely warnings: a siren for residents and SMS notifications for officials.

Context
Ukhiya Rohingya Camp
Type
Rainfall early-warning device
Alerts
On-site siren & SMS to officials
  • IoT
  • Rain gauge
  • Rainfall monitoring
  • Siren alarm
  • SMS alerts
  • Early warning

Overview

Landslides in hill areas are usually triggered by sustained heavy rain. This device watches rainfall where it matters, so residents and officials get a warning while there is still time to act, rather than after the slope has already moved.

How it works

  • Rainfall monitoring: a rain gauge continuously measures rainfall levels in the landslide-prone area.
  • Early detection: the device recognises critical rainfall conditions as they develop.
  • Siren for residents: a loud siren alerts people living in the area on site.
  • SMS to officials: SMS notifications reach the responsible officials so they can take proactive measures.

Circuit diagrams and source code are confidential to the client.
