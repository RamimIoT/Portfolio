Environment & Wind Monitor with Toxic Gas Neutralizer

A solar-powered ESP32 station that detects toxic gases such as ammonia and chlorine and neutralizes them with a servo-driven neutralizer, while monitoring temperature, humidity, sunlight intensity and wind speed.

Type
Environmental monitoring & toxic gas neutralizer
Platform
ESP32 Dev Kit v1, Wi-Fi
Power
Solar tracking with battery backup
  • ESP32
  • MQ-135
  • Wind speed sensor
  • DHT11
  • Servo
  • Solar tracking
  • 16x2 LCD
  • Wi-Fi

Overview

The system monitors toxic gases such as ammonia and chlorine and neutralizes them, while also tracking environmental conditions: temperature, humidity, sunlight intensity and wind speed. It runs on solar power with a tracking mount and battery backup, so it can operate as a self-contained station.

How it works

  • Toxic gas sensing: an MQ-135 air quality sensor detects ammonia (NH3) and other toxic gases.
  • Gas neutralizer: a servo-driven neutralizer with 180-degree movement neutralizes the detected gas.
  • Weather monitoring: a wind speed sensor measures wind, a DHT11 reads temperature and humidity, and sunlight intensity is monitored as well.
  • Local display: a 16x2 LCD shows system information.
  • Connectivity: the ESP32's built-in Wi-Fi connects the system to the internet.

Hardware

  • Microcontroller: ESP32 Dev Kit v1 (built-in Wi-Fi)
  • Sensors: MQ-135 air quality sensor (ammonia), wind speed sensor, DHT11 temperature & humidity sensor
  • Actuator: toxic gas neutralizer on a servo with 180-degree movement
  • Display: 16x2 LCD
  • Power: solar tracking system, power management system and battery backup
